<h2 class="subtitle">Abstract</h2>

For 27&thinsp;000 days, under the banner of Sumud, the Palestinians were trying to recover their patrimony taken away from them by the Zionist invasion of Palestine while under the protection of the British Mandate. The paper exposes many facts not known or denied exposure, such as: the Zionist military invasion of Palestine took place before Israel was declared, massacres were a weapon of ethnic cleansing, Israel waged 12 kinds of wars against the Palestinians, the Western powers that supported Israel and violated international law are the same who did it for one thousand years, the Right of Return is sacred, legal, feasible and inevitable. Geographic and demographic plans will be shown to explain the feasibility of the Return. What is the real motive behind October 7? Finally, the blood of over 100&thinsp;000 killed and injured Palestinians in Gaza recently, what does it mean to the future, the region and the world? <a href="#fn1" id="fn1back" class="backlink"><sup>i</sup></a>

Thank you for inviting me. Thank you, Prof Claus Detlev Quintern and colleagues, for your perseverance in holding this conference, in spite of the insidious campaign to silence the voice of academia.  It is unbelievable that this could happen in the age of internet, social media and the UN. It is a disgrace to those who practice it and an honor to those who resist it.
Palestinians have been subject to the most savage kind of colonization by the strangest kind of colonizers. Zionism is a peculiar kind of European colonialism. Colonial projects usually have a singular capital and a country to return to. Colonial countries have their own language, geography, history and culture. Zionism had none of these. They had to invent a virtual parallel to the Western projects, none of it is genuine. They have to erase Palestine’s land and people from history, geography and memory.
To start with, the Zionist project had no home country. European Jews, or Ashkenazis, were constantly moving in Europe, since they converted to Judaism in Al Khazar Kingdom in the eighth century. Many immigrated to America in the early decades of the twentieth century. You find them today in AIPAC, running the US Congress.
In the 19th century in Europe, they lived in ghettoes, either by force or by choice. During the Emancipation, when Napoleon tried to demolish ghetto walls, they refused to identify themselves as nationals of the country of their domicile.
Thus, it became a survival principle of Zionism to invent their own history, geography and culture. This needed a great deal of imagination and power of deception, hanging by a thin thread of credibility.
The first Zionist conference at Basel in 1897 was attended by wealthy European Jews, who wanted to create a colony for themselves, in parallel with European colonies, which they financed. Their project needed simple people to serve the cause. Hence the reincarnation of the Biblical Israel by the atheist Zionists. It was a sure way to collect recruits among poor European Jews.
The geographical Israel of the Bible was created virtually on the land of Palestine. 
With that, the Zionist colonial project had a people, the Ashkenazis, and a land in Palestine which they called Israel. What remains is to conquer this land. But this land is the home of Palestinians for thousands of years; it is charted on all maps for centuries. How could they conquer it?
Zionists solved the problem by claiming it was empty. Hence the slogan: Palestine is a land without people and Jews are a people without land. A perfect match.
The geography of the colony is now settled. Next comes history. The story is told that the so-called citizens of this country in Palestine decided to abandon it for more than 2000 years, then they decided to return to repossess their patrimony. 
Big story. A long shot of disbelief.
How about the language. All European Jews spoke the languages of their domicile. They searched for a new language. Roman churches pray in Latin, although Europeans do not speak it.  Old Hebrew, the language of Jewish prayers, was adopted as the new language of the new country. Since it lacked expressions for modern technology, these were devised and inserted into it.
How about the recruits of the new country? They are Russians, Polish, Hungarian, Romanian and Ukrainian Jews. They posed as the original Palestinians.
For credibility, they had to adopt Palestinian food like Maqlouba and falafel. They had to wear Palestinian clothes. They found the rich Palestinian embroidery to select from. This is a unique page in the history of imposters.
On the ground in Palestine, they attacked the Palestinians to expel them, to make Palestine without people. They attacked them by a formidable army, depopulated 530 cities and villages and made its people refugees today.. That was not enough. Constant wars are waged against them, in every camp wherever they are. The ethnic cleansing was elevated to the level of genocide, to the level of erasing their existence all together.
On the world scene, this huge deception of an invented people and an invented country needed a big, powerful machine to prop it up.
There were two arenas for this false ideology to prevail:
The first arena: a military campaign in Palestine, Lebanon and the whole region, to kill, destroy and demolish all signs of life. Hence the genocide of Gaza people and the assassination of the resistance leaders. You saw this very well on your TV screens.
The second arena: a deception campaign, a propaganda campaign of huge proportions. Its main field is the Western countries, where they have big power. In the deception campaign, a two-pronged plan was devised in complementary ways: attack and defence.
The attack is to unleash an army of movies, university courses, curricula, institutes, newspaper articles, books and sharp-tongued spokesmen, to sell this narrative. The defence is to prohibit, punish and banish all those who tell the truth. The way to do this is to create an army of watchers, to monitor the deniers, to hound and silence them. 
The aim of this campaign is to bury the truth, vilify its proponents and silence its speakers. In other words, to kill the speaking human conscience. 
Zionism waged 12 kinds of wars against us, the Palestinians.
The first war is military, which you are familiar with. 
As you know, the army of settlers, which attacked Palestine in 1948, consisted of 120,000 soldiers in 9 brigades and carried out 38 military operations. They attacked and depopulated 530 Palestinian towns and villages and made its people refugees till today. They committed 356 war crimes. This war did not stop for 76 years. The Genocide in Gaza is enacted before our eyes daily. The blood of 200,000 killed and injured Palestinians (and the number is still rising) is known around the world.
The second war is silencing the voice of the victims. In 1948, about six dozen major massacres took place. None was reported by the five Western news agencies at the time, except perhaps the massacre of Deir Yassin. 

The UN reported the most horrendous massacre at Ed Dawayma in October 1948, where 600 people were savagely killed. In a formal letter to the UN, Abba Eban asserted that Dawayima does not exist.

Tzipi Hotovely, the past Israel’s UK ambassador, called Al Nakba an “Arab lie”. Mention of al Nakba is outlawed in Israel. This truth is still denied in many US universities at the pain of cutting off its funds. Your conference today is an example of the attempt to silence the voice of truth. You are witnesses to that.
 
The third war is geographical. Our “Atlas of Palestine 1917-1966” lists 50,000 names, carved by the people about their life and places from olden times. An Israeli Naming Committee deleted all these Palestinian names and replaced them with 6,800 created names. The settlers themselves shed their European names and adopted Hebrew names.
 
The fourth war is historical. The historical record of Palestine in the last 2,000 years is obliterated and made blank in Israeli textbooks.  It is replaced by the history of settler Jews who arrived in Palestine after the First World War. Two thousand years of Palestine history, for which historical edifices are still abound, are totally deleted from record.
 
The fifth war is archaeological. The destruction of over 500 Palestinian villages after 1948 was carried out by bulldozers of the Jewish National Fund (JNF) and Public Works.  Strangely, an Israeli archaeological team supervised the demolition and ordered the destruction of any historical buildings, Byzantine, Arab and Islamic monuments, and any other unless they find in them something proving Jewish history.
How did the Palestinian landscape look like after the destruction of hundreds of villages? A pile of stones where a house once stood. A lone standing wall of a mosque. A cracked bell tower of a church.  A marble piece of a grave epitaph. This evil scene must be hidden. The JNF covered the crime by planting trees over them, called parks. These parks are named after leaders of Western countries who supported Zionism.
  
The sixth war is political. Next to military war, this is the most effective. The US and many Western countries vote regularly against UN resolutions condemning Israel’s crimes and violations of international law. They thwart any legal action against Israel. You have already seen the USA voting against a ceasefire several times as well as against the ICC and the ICJ, allowing the genocide to continue. 
 
The seventh war is religious. God is recruited to win this war. Zionists claim that they have the ‘right’ to return, after a 2,000-year absence, to the God-given land, to recover their property and to expel the “natives”. The problem was that Ben Gurion was an atheist but this narrative was good for the cause. The Evangelical Christians, Trump’s main supporters --and the former Restorationists of the 19th century-- are recruited to support Zionism, with great effect in the United States.
 
The eighth war is defamation. Palestinian fighters and supporters are called “terrorists”. Edward Said was called ‘a professor of terror’. Anyone who explains the facts about Palestine, even among respected scholars or UN officials, is called an anti-Semite, The Zionist-drafted IHRA document is pushed for adoption by UK universities. Your meeting today may discuss the defamation, harassment and even expulsion of those academics who dare to tell the truth.

The ninth war is educational. Israel set up many well-funded outfits to monitor any courses of education which affirm the identity of Palestinian school children. The UNRWA is forced to monitor schoolbooks and fire any teacher who tells children who they are, where they come from, why they are refugees and why they have the right to return home. Israel wants to wipe out Palestine from the curriculum. I have a long battle with the UNRWA education department under threat of cutting funds.
Other Zionist monitoring machines are UN Watch, Campus Watch, CAMERA, Honest Reporting, Impact SE and MEMRI.  
 
The tenth war is lawfare. Domestic law in some Western countries criminalizes support for the grassroots campaign of boycott, divestment and sanctions (BDS) and hence any attempt to criticize Israeli war crimes and Apartheid. There is a witch hunt, in USA and Germany particularly, against any mention in Academia of Israeli crimes against Palestinians. 
 
The eleventh war is economic. Jewish financial power in the United States, supporting AIPAC, is a potent force. This power influences the US government by regular payments to the Congress and the media in ways never known in any country in history.  
 
The twelfth war is genocide. It is the most sinister of all. It aims at erasing the whole presence of the Palestinian people. The world watches the daily slaughter of women and children in Gaza, the indiscriminate bombing of holders of communication devices. The world watches this on TV and in the halls of the ICJ. Every line of the Genocide Convention is violated. 

This is the blackest era in the history of Zionism and the West.  

To top it all, ponder the big campaign for the hostages to return home. I am for the return of hostages without any hesitation. Let the hostages go home wherever they belong. But I am against ugly racism, hailing one hostage, ignoring another hostage. ALL hostages should go home where they belong. 

Nobody speaks of the REAL long-time hostages. They are two million Palestinian refugees in the Gaza Concentration Camp, from 247 towns and villages in southern Palestine. expelled by Israel in 1948, through dozens of massacres. They are crammed in a concentration camp called Gaza Strip at a density of 8000 persons/km2. Its area is 1.3% of Palestine, or 365 km2. They are now forced by Israel to move down south, then up north in the tiny strip at a density exceeding 20,000 p/km2. 

Who occupies their home now? East European settlers from Romania, Poland, Ukraine and Russia. Their number is only 150,000, at a density of only 7 persons/km2, one thousand times less than the owners of the land, who are the refugees in Gaza.

So, I ask you: who are the real hostages? Are they the 100 European settlers held in Gaza for 300 days? Or are they the two million Palestinian refugees held in Gaza camps attacked by land, air and sea and blockaded for 76 years, or 27,000 days?
